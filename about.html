About DigiWise

DigiWise blends personalized learning with digital well‑being tools to help students learn smarter and live healthier online lives.

We focus on: reducing digital addiction, teaching cyber safety, supporting teachers & parents, and making learning more engaging through gamification and interactive lessons.

👩‍🏫

Teachers

Tools to create lessons, quizzes and monitor class progress.

🧑‍🎓

Students

Multiple learning formats and an AI chat companion for friendly guidance.

👪

Parents

Dashboards and parental controls for healthy digital habits.

Objectives

  • Engaging and safe learning environment
  • Reduce digital addiction via mindful reminders
  • Teach cyber safety through interactive simulations
  • Personalized learning for different styles
